## Tells that it's AI-written

Re-read this list before shipping any content and delete anything that matches:

- Generic AI-tell phrases: "unlock", "leverage", "seamless", "world-class", "in today's fast-paced world", "dive into", "game-changer", "elevate"
- Exclamation marks
- Emojis in body copy
- Three-item lists where two would do
- A closing paragraph that restates the opening instead of adding anything new
- Hedging that isn't actually uncertain ("it's important to note that")
